Biology and transmission of coccidian parasites

We use different imaging and biophysical approaches combined to classic in vitro culture and molecular techniques to study how coccidian parasites persist in the environments and infect their hosts. We hope that our works will help to better prevent and control coccidian-related infections in humans and/or animals.
